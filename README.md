# ไปด้วยกัน — Bpai Duay Gan

*"Go together."* A Chiang Mai app that pairs Thai locals with nomads and expats so nobody has to walk into an event alone, and so both sides have something to give.

Built at the Claude Community Hackathon, Chiang Mai, 26–27 September 2026.

## What it does

- **Events in your language.** Local Thai events and nomad workshops, gathered from the platforms where each community already posts, shown in Thai or English.
- **Event Buddy.** Pick an event, tap *Need a buddy*, and get matched with someone from the other community who wants to go too.
- **Skill & Sabai swaps.** Everyone registers what they can give and what they want to learn. Locals offer Kham Mueang basics, street food knowledge, help with bureaucracy. Foreigners offer English conversation, tech, remote-work know-how. Matches run both ways.
- **Chat across the language line.** Each person writes in their own language. Translation adapts for politeness and cultural context, so English arrives as friendly Thai and Thai arrives as natural English.
